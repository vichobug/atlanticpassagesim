Atlantic Passage Sim

A Monte Carlo passage-time and provisioning calculator for the ARC Atlantic crossing — Las Palmas (Gran Canaria) to Rodney Bay (St Lucia), ~2,590 nm, simulated on a Hallberg-Rassy 40 using ten years of real ERA5 wind reanalysis data, sampled as full historical passages rather than independent daily draws.

5,000 simulated trials · real historical wind, not synthetic · source on GitHub

Passage-time distribution

Each trial picks a random historical Nov–Jan start date from 2015–2025 and sails the route day by day through that real wind sequence, so multi-day weather persistence (lulls, squally patches) comes through naturally.

Methodology notes

  • Boat: Hallberg-Rassy 40, real polar (speed) table, 2D-interpolated for arbitrary wind angle/strength.
  • Wind: ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus Climate Data Store), 2015–2025, daily-mean 10m u/v components.
  • Route: rhumb-line geometry, ~2,590 nm, course ~251° true.
  • Consumption rates: 4 L water + 1.8 kg food per person per day (bluewater-cruising rules of thumb); fuel is a boat-level 12.5 L/day rate reflecting the commonly-cited ARC pattern of motoring 10–20% of the crossing through calms.

Full source, data pipeline, and write-up: github.com/vichobug/atlanticpassagesim
